About · Story

Building AI that decides, not just predicts

I'm a Senior AI Engineer specializing in intelligent, decentralized AI systems that power real-world decisions. Most recently at Digital Currency Group, I architected production-ready AI agents with models like Qwen, LLaMA, Gemma, and Phi, orchestrated with LangChain, LangGraph, and Weaviate.

One of my proudest projects is VCScout-AI, an end-to-end autonomous agent that analyzed 300+ startup deals and directly drove $450K+ in AI-informed investments.

Before DCG, I led TensorFlow CPU performance optimization at Intel and earned the Division Achievement Award. I hold an MS in Data Science from Northeastern University.
